# K-LINE MEN Catalog — V3.1 Assessment

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Branch assessed:** `feature/v3-coherence` (local, not pushed)
**Question answered:** Against [V3_audit instruction.md](V3_audit%20instruction.md), is the premium endstate fully achieved, or can the UI/UX and workflows be pushed further toward a high-class product?
**Method:** Code-grounded verification of every claim in the [V3_ROADMAP.md §10 progress log](V3_ROADMAP.md), plus a fresh premium re-audit across the instruction's ten lenses. Every finding was re-checked against the actual source (false positives and overstatements dropped). Pairs with [UX_PRODUCT_AUDIT.md](UX_PRODUCT_AUDIT.md) (the v2 diagnosis) and [V3_ROADMAP.md](V3_ROADMAP.md) (the prescription).

> This is a reference/roadmap document. Nothing was modified to produce it.

---

## 0. Verdict

**The endstate is _not_ fully achieved — but the build is an honest, high-quality partial (~60% of the way there).**

V3 delivered its two highest-value bets cleanly (burst capture; IA slim-down) and a real quick-win sweep. But of its own six exit criteria ([V3_ROADMAP.md §6](V3_ROADMAP.md)), only ~1.5 are fully met, and the audit's single most important finding — the perceived-speed gap (C1) — was **masked, not solved**. The app moved from "capable operator's tool" to "well-organized capable tool." It has **not** crossed into "a first-time user recognizes it as high-end within 30 seconds."

The remaining 40% is concentrated and largely known: one genuinely hard item (the render path), a cluster of cheap safety/touch fixes, three half-finished coherence threads, the deferred design-system tokens — plus one net-new AI-forward move that would do more for perceived quality than any amount of polish.

> **Note on the roadmap's self-report:** the top-of-file "Every phase below was implemented" banner **overclaims**. The detailed §10 progress log is honest by omission (it lists only what shipped). Three Phase-3 scope goals and most of Phases 4–6 were never completed.

## 4. What still blocks "premium" — ranked

### A. Perceived speed (the headline gap) — _Critical_
C1 is the #1 audit finding and is **confirmed unresolved** ([gallery.js:998-999](../../src/gallery.js#L998-L999)). `content-visibility` defers offscreen paint but the full HTML string build + DOM reparse of 1,000 cards still blocks the main thread per keystroke. This is the line between "competent" and "Linear-fast."
**Fix:** incremental/windowed render, or build-once-then-filter-in-place (hide/show existing nodes). ~150 LOC, real integration risk to selection/sweep — the one genuinely hard item.

### B. Workflow coherence half-finished — _High_
- **Pricing** still opens 3 tools from one "Set prices" label with no signposting ([gallery.js:1158-1160](../../src/gallery.js#L1158-L1160), [:1811](../../src/gallery.js#L1811)); cost still lives only in the pivot tool.
- **Sync counts** computed two ways ([shop.js:50-56](../../src/shop.js#L50-L56) vs [synccenter.js:67-81](../../src/synccenter.js#L67-L81)) — divergence risk.
- **Activity is invisible plumbing** — no global home; only per-item history in the editor ([editor.js](../../src/editor.js)). Phase 3 and Phase 6 both promised it.
- **Swipe-review stack** never built — Review is still tap-one-at-a-time ([gallery.js:1971](../../src/gallery.js#L1971)); the readiness data is already plumbed ([readiness.js:92-161](../../src/readiness.js#L92-L161), [editor.js:806-813](../../src/editor.js#L806-L813)).

### C. Touch / a11y / data-safety quick-wins — _High value, Low effort_
- Temp password is plain `type="text"` ([users.js:53](../../src/users.js#L53)) — **shoulder-surf security gap**.
- Ref-data queries lack `.limit()` ([data.js:13-18](../../src/data.js#L13-L18)) — **silent truncation at Supabase's 1,000-row default**.
- Bottom-nav 40px ([styles.css:238-243](../../src/styles.css#L238-L243)) / sheet-foot 42px ([styles.css:929](../../src/styles.css#L929)); `.lc` inline confidence is hover-only ([gallery.js:80](../../src/gallery.js#L80)) on the primary (touch) platform.
- AI-Suggest marks checkboxes "changed" without changing their saved value ([editor.js:555](../../src/editor.js#L555) assigns `.value`, not `.checked`) — cosmetic, **not** the data-overwrite the v2 audit reported. Open question: skip checkboxes cleanly, or let AI tick unchecked ones.
- Calibration `onNext` advances even when the per-item mark save errors ([calibration.js:189](../../src/calibration.js#L189), [:208](../../src/calibration.js#L208)) — a failed write silently loses that item's verdicts with no retry. (Per-item checkpointing already exists; the gap is error handling, not save cadence.)

### D. Design system deferred — _Medium now, High long-term_
Confirmed real and compounding: 86 radius uses across 10 values (`--radius` honored once), 16 font sizes (incl. fractional `10.5/11.5/12.5px`), no spacing tokens, 5+ parallel chip systems, 2 bottom-sheet systems, 4 segmented controls, re-rolled primary buttons. Invisible to users, but it's _why_ the app reads "assembled from parts." Token scales are low-risk (mechanical find-replace).

### E. States & motion uneven — _Medium_
Bare error toasts that delete the modal with no retry ([pricing.js:72](../../src/pricing.js#L72)); missing skeletons on pricing/shop/calibration; only two motion effects total. Phase 6's "loading states on every async surface" shipped ~21 lines.

## 5. The bigger leap — net-new premium the roadmap never scoped

V3 explicitly froze AI ("no new AI capabilities," [§3](V3_ROADMAP.md)) and never examined **first-run** or **PWA-native** surfaces. These are where the app vaults past "polished" into "obviously intelligent."

1. **Auto-fire AI in burst + live filmstrip ID.** Make AI the default in burst (it's an opt-in checkbox today, [upload.js:185](../../src/upload.js#L185)/[:474](../../src/upload.js#L474)); stream each thumbnail flipping spinner → "Dior Sauvage · EDP · 100ml" + confidence dot. **The single biggest missing high-end moment** — snap a rack, watch it identify itself.
2. **AI price suggestion** as a ghost value in guided pricing — the extractor already resolves exact product identity, yet pricing is 100% manual keypad entry (the one workflow with zero intelligence and the highest cognitive load).
3. **First-run coached demo** — the empty catalog ([gallery.js:560-568](../../src/gallery.js#L560-L568)) is a dead-end emoji; turn it into a 30-second "snap your first unit and watch it fill" loop. Onboarding was missed by every lens; the bare login ([login.js](../../src/login.js)) has no value-prop.
4. **PWA `shortcuts` + `share_target`** ([vite.config.js](../../vite.config.js)) — installed-icon "Burst capture / Review" quick actions, and accept photos shared from the camera roll. Cheapest "feels like a real app" upgrade; pure manifest + a small receiving route.
5. **Near-duplicate guard at capture** — under one-photo-one-unit, an accidental re-shoot silently inflates stock; a cheap client-side image hash catches it at the moment it happens (today duplicates are only found after the fact, [consistency.js:104](../../src/consistency.js#L104)).
6. **Proactive alerts** (opt-in badge / Web Push) for "shop sync failing" / "N new items to review" — the data exists ([gallery.js:558](../../src/gallery.js#L558)); only the outbound nudge is missing.

**Recommended sequence:** P0 quick-wins first (build trust, near-zero risk), then choose between the **C1 render fix** (closes the headline gap) and the **auto-AI burst moment** (biggest perceived-quality jump) as the flagship of V3.1 — these are the two that most move the "is this high-end?" needle.

---

## 7. Bottom line

V3 did the hard, risky things well and was honest about what it deferred. It is **~60% of the way** to the stated premium endstate. Almost no _new_ features are required to finish the original vision — the remaining work is **finishing four deferred threads + a handful of cheap safety/touch fixes**. The one optional leap beyond the original scope (AI-forward capture + first-run) is what would make the product read as _intelligent_, not just _organized_.
