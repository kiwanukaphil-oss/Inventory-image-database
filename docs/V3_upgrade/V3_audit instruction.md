**Act as a world-class product designer, UX architect, mobile app design specialist, interaction designer, and usability expert.**

Please conduct a comprehensive review of the application, focusing not only on visual design but also on the complete user experience, workflows, information architecture, interaction patterns, and overall product polish.

Assume the goal is to create a **premium, modern, mobile-first application** that feels comparable to the quality and refinement of the best consumer and SaaS products in the market.

Evaluate the application from the following perspectives:

### 1. Visual Design & Aesthetics

- Overall visual quality and first impressions
- Modernity and uniqueness of the design
- Brand identity and consistency
- Typography hierarchy and readability
- Color system and accessibility
- Spacing, alignment, and visual balance
- Iconography and imagery
- Professionalism and perceived product quality

### 2. Mobile-First Experience

- How well the application works on mobile devices
- Thumb-friendly navigation
- Reachability of critical actions
- Mobile ergonomics and usability
- Responsiveness across screen sizes
- Efficiency of common mobile workflows
- Areas where desktop patterns have been incorrectly carried over to mobile

### 3. User Experience & Interaction Design

- Simplicity and intuitiveness
- Cognitive load placed on users
- Ease of learning for first-time users
- Friction points and unnecessary complexity
- Quality of interactions and micro-interactions
- User feedback and system responsiveness
- Error prevention and recovery
- Empty states, loading states, and success states

### 4. Workflow Analysis

Review every major workflow end-to-end and identify:

- Unnecessary steps
- Repeated actions
- Context switching
- Opportunities for automation
- Opportunities for smart defaults
- Opportunities for AI assistance
- Workflow bottlenecks
- Areas where users must think too much

For each workflow, recommend:

- A better workflow
- Reduced click/tap count
- Faster completion paths
- Improved user guidance
- Better information presentation

### 5. Information Architecture

- Navigation structure
- Menu organization
- Screen hierarchy
- Discoverability of features
- Clarity of terminology
- Grouping of related functionality
- Overall logical flow of the application

### 6. Premium Product Experience

Evaluate whether the application feels:

- Enterprise-grade
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Fast
- Intelligent
- Modern
- Delightful to use

Identify elements that make the product feel:

- Cheap
- Generic
- Dated
- Overly complex
- Unprofessional

### 7. High-End App Benchmarking

Compare the experience against the design quality standards of products such as:

- Linear
- Notion
- Stripe Dashboard
- Revolut
- Monzo
- Uber

Identify gaps between the current application and these best-in-class experiences.

### 8. Design System Review

Assess:

- Component consistency
- Reusable patterns
- Form design
- Tables and lists
- Cards
- Navigation components
- Buttons
- Dialogs and drawers
- Search experiences
- Filters
- Data visualization

Recommend improvements that would create a cohesive design system.

### 9. Mobile Workflow Innovation

Do not limit recommendations to conventional business software.

Propose:

- Novel interaction patterns
- Gesture-based workflows
- Bottom-sheet experiences
- Contextual actions
- AI-assisted workflows
- Smart shortcuts
- Command palettes
- Quick actions
- Automation opportunities

The goal is to create a product experience that feels significantly more modern than traditional business software.

### 10. Deliverables

Provide:

1. Critical issues that require immediate attention
2. High-impact UX improvements
3. Mobile-first redesign recommendations
4. Workflow redesign recommendations
5. Premium experience enhancements
6. Quick wins with high ROI
7. Features that should be removed or simplified
8. Screens that should be redesigned
9. Components that should be standardized
10. A prioritized roadmap ranked by impact versus implementation effort

Do not provide generic design advice. Critique the application as if you were preparing it for launch as a world-class commercial product competing against the best software products in the market.

The objective is to create a **sleek, highly polished, mobile-first experience with exceptional workflows, minimal friction, strong visual hierarchy, delightful interactions, and a premium feel that users immediately recognize as a high-end product.**
